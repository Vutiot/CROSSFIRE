On December 9, 2021 at approximately 2:08 pm, COPA was notified via a telephone call
from the Chicago Police Department Bureau of Internal Affairs that officers were responding to a
possible officer-involved domestic homicide at the home of who with
Officer Pierre Tyler.2 COPA’s investigation revealed that officers responded to
residence to conduct a well-being check on after she failed to pick up her children from
the homes of relatives. Upon their arrival, they found deceased. It was later determined
that sustained a gunshot wound and her death was ruled a homicide. The evidence
revealed that Officer Tyler was captured on video as the last person entering and exiting her
residence. Officer Tyler was eventually arrested and charged with First Degree Murder for the
death of COPA’s investigation finds that the allegations, that Officer Tyler discharged a
firearm at without justification, failed to report that he discharged a firearm, failed to
secure a firearm registered to him and was absent from work without permission, are Sustained.
