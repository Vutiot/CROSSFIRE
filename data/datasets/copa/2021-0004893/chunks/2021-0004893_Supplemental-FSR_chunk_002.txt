II. SUMMARY OF EVIDENCE
According to the Chicago Police Department’s Attendance and Assignment Record,4 on
December 8, 2021, Officer Pierre Tyler was assigned to work 11:30 am to 8:30 pm. The records
showed that Officer Tyler was listed as being present for work and assigned to Beat 1562C and
Car #4909. According to Sgt. Theophilo Kerkeres, documentation for time off is completed by the
requesting officer in an automated system.5 There is no indication in the Attendance and
Assignment Record or any other evidence obtained that Officer Tyler submitted necessary
documentation for time off, or that he adjusted his hours on December 8, 2021.
