RECORD OF CONVERSATION
Investigator-1
Air Safety Investigator
Western Pacific Region
Date: March 25, 2019
Person Contacted: Witness-3 (Airline-A Airport-Charlie Base Manager)
NTSB Accident Number: CASE-ANON-001
Narrative:
The following is a synopsis of the information provided by Witness-3 in a telephone
conversation on this date:
- Witness-3 is the Airline-A Base Manager at Airport-Charlie
- Her Safety Manager is Witness-4
- Airline-A at Airport-Charlie is a "satellite" of the main facility in Location-15, Region-Southeast
- To her knowledge, the accident pilot/airplane departed Airport-Charlie about 1530 PST
- After the accident, she heard that the airplane flew to Airport-Alpha, where it picked up a female
passenger
- This passenger pickup was not accomplished in accordance with Airline-A policies and procedures
Rental pilots were required to list all passengers, as well as passenger emergency
o
contact information, with Airline-A prior to any flight
- The pilot had accumulated about 31 hours of flight time at Airline-A Airport-Charlie, all in the previous
few weeks
- The pilot had acquired most of his training and flight time in an unknown facility (not the
Airline-A parent company) in Location-15, Region-Southeast
- The pilot stated his objective was "to build time"
- About a week prior to the accident, the pilot had shown up at Airline-A to conduct a flight, but
then cancelled due to weather
- At that time, he had a female with him, but Witness-3 did not know her name, or whether
this was the same person as the accident passenger
- Witness-3 relayed a witness account of the pilot attempting to start the accident airplane
at Airport-Alpha while it was still tied down
- The NTSB requested and was provided with that witness's contact information
