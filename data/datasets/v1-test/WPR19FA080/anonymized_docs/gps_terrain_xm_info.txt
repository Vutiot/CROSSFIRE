Dear Investigator-1,
GPS Aera 660 in N-XXXX was not connected to any XM weather receiver, it was only wired to
audiopanel to relay warning messages to intercom/pilot headset. Warnings/messages were
enabled for terrain or airspace, and user selected track points and crew was getting them via
audio input to audiopanel and their headset. However terrain and other warning features of
Aera 660 are not to be used as primary source of information, they have only secondary
information character - Aera 660 is not certified TAWS system.
See chapter 4.2 (page 119) of Garmin Aera 660 manual for terrain warning details:
http://static.garmin.com/pumac/190-02017-20_h.pdf
